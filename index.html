Questions

Good to know

How much of our time does this take?

Very little. Most of the work happens on our side. You fill in one questionnaire, review the documents, and join one short call per deliverable. Ongoing support is by email.

Who actually does the work?

A senior Sorvanti strategist, using a proven method and our own AI-assisted tools to work quickly. Every recommendation is reviewed by a person before it reaches you.

Do you set up the tools too?

We decide what's worth doing and write a clear brief. Your IT provider or a contractor builds it, or our Transformation team can for larger projects. That way you're never paying strategy rates for setup work.

Is our customer data safe?

Data rules are the first thing we set. We never need access to your systems or sensitive records to do our work.

What does it cost?

Each package has a fixed price, agreed before any work starts. There's no hourly billing, and the monthly plan has no long contract: cancel anytime.
